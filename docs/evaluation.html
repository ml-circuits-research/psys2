Evaluation

Status: planned (vision only) Only visible per-world competency tests exist today.

Specs: none (vision only)

Vision: vision §15

Purpose

How PSys2 decides that something works. Evaluation separates formalization, reasoning over given circuits, end-to-end answers, autonomous learning and operational behavior; no single F1 replaces these. Every metric declares denominator, dataset lineage, language, domain, hardware and budget, with confidence intervals and sample counts; small samples are provisional evidence only. Thresholds are initial policy values; changes are versioned and cannot be made silently by the agent being evaluated (vision §15).

The fixture implementation has only tests.sop competency tests per world (Worlds) plus npm test; protected sets, calibration and the targets below are not yet measured.

Measurement areas

AreaWhat is measured
FormalizationMention and type accuracy, role-conditioned span F1, reference accuracy, scope accuracy, query-plan accuracy, full-circuit semantic agreement (after canonicalization); structural validity reported separately; negative and ambiguous cases (negation, conditions, quoted claims, multiple entities, conflicting revisions, missing inputs)
Selective answersAnswer coverage = answered eligible / all eligible; selective error = incorrect answered / answered; also incorrect / all and abstention-reason accuracy. Proof status never sets interpretation confidence
Autonomous construction (C1)Held-out world-building tasks: completion rate, coverage, unresolved capabilities, agent and GPU cost, independent answers where available; machine-generated tests alone cannot show real-world accuracy
Autonomous improvement (C3)Streams with known failure families, new sources and unanswerable cases: corrected errors, coverage gain, unsupported corrections, regressions, time to resolution; a valid cycle may retract a belief or find evidence insufficient
Scale and composition1, 10 and 100 worlds: router recall, unknown-world rejection, cross-world context errors, adapter load time, cache memory, tail latency, training interference; cold and warm; cross-world identity collisions, incompatible revisions, retractions, default invalidation
Gate integrityRelease service owns protected tests and thresholds; candidate fixes its regression, keeps critical invariants, meets coverage contract, stays within tolerance
Proof and executionApplicability, dimensional correctness, domain constraints, result verification: count properties and assumptions; inject crashes, exhausted budgets, denied capabilities, partial writes, stale imports

Initial quantitative targets

Targets are separated from guarantees. Missing one creates a recorded research or engineering task; it does not authorize generative formalization.

ComponentInitial target
EncoderRole-conditioned extraction F1 at least 90%; scope and reference accuracy at least 95% each on the world's protected set; report full-circuit accuracy separately
Graph assemblyAt least 95% structurally valid first-pass circuits; no silent scope repair; all invalid cases classified
RouterCorrect world in top three for at least 95% of in-scope segments at 100 worlds; out-of-domain false admission at most 5% on a separately reported set
Accepted worldAt least 70% answer coverage and at most 2% selective error on declared eligible question families; provisional status when sample is insufficient
RegressionNo critical invariant failures; at most 2 percentage points loss in unchanged noncritical metrics; all absolute error limits still met
C1 automationAt least 8 of 10 benchmark world-build jobs within recorded budgets, with no human labeling or manual artifact edits
C3 improvementOver three evaluation cycles, reduce targeted errors or raise useful coverage in aggregate; no critical regressions; no unsupported correction promoted
C2 latencyOn a named GPU host: median under 2 s, p95 under 5 s for a 256-token request, up to three candidate worlds, 24 key queries per world, reasoning over 100,000 indexed wires
Scale100 registered worlds, bounded adapter cache; warm accuracy within regression tolerance of isolated execution; cold load reported separately
Audit and recoveryComplete lineage for every promoted change; crash-safe publication and rollback in all fault-injection cases

Long documents, solver-heavy queries and external actions have separate latency classes. CPU inference is benchmarked on a named machine and reported independently; a GPU result is never a CPU claim. The caps above are benchmark definitions, not architectural limits. The million-wire benchmark is a separate indexed-query experiment, not a prerequisite for C1 automation and no promise of sub-100 ms arbitrary reasoning.

Fixture worlds and targets

Per ADR-001, status=fixture worlds do not count toward C1 acceptance targets. A world's tests.sop checks competency on visible cases; it is not a protected acceptance set. Rule-derived training labels are a separate label status from corroborated or generator-backed ones.

Failure modes

  • The agent re-inspects a hidden set repeatedly: the set is retired and replaced from independent families.
  • Sample too small: reported as provisional, not as a safety claim.
  • Target missed: a recorded task; the architecture does not fall back to generative formalization.
