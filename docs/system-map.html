3. Three numbers per world

Names: a world schema says what a kind of world is (kinds, attributes, acts, limits, words; no particular things); a knowledge world holds facts true for everyone (k.*, no session state); a live world is one running instance of a world schema in a session (the things the user made, focus, history), shown in a view (a tab); library modules (std.*) are mechanisms, not worlds.

Hand-written lines: non-blank, non-comment lines of the world's .sop and .mjs files, tests and generated files left out. Reuse: of the types the world uses, the share that comes from elsewhere (the library's report, docs/library-reuse-report.md; "-" when the report has no row). Turns and helped: turns the world answered on the logs, and the share where another world or the knowledge catalog was asked (a fill, lookup, ask or layout edge fired; opening a tab, mirroring a view and routing are plumbing and do not count).

4. One vocabulary for the trace

The trace of a turn (reply.trace, shown in Developer mode) has a steps list with the seven steps above, in this order. The older trace fields map to them as follows:

  • kind of turn: trace.discourse.meta and the dialogue log's kind (new, continuation, why, correction, undo ...)
  • world: trace.routing (tierName: modal, active-tab, recent, priority, meta.worlds) and trace.router
  • act: trace.patterns
  • things: trace.segments[].match.slots
  • what is missing and who fills it: trace.discourse.pending and the dialogue log's filled (role, value, source)
  • apply: trace.operations and trace.effects
  • reply: trace.replyEnglish and trace.statuses

Known mismatches (left as they are because renaming them would change replies that tests and logs depend on):

  • The "why" answer says "I read it as a request for the X because it is the tab that was open"; that is step 2 (world). It has no sentence for step 3 (act) or step 4 (things), and the kind of turn is only said for corrections.
  • "came from the scene / what we had just talked about / the usual words for that kind of request" is step 5 (what is missing and who fills it); the tiers are named focus, scene, knowledge in the data and said differently in the sentence.
  • "What I answered" is step 7 (reply); the offer to undo is step 6 (apply) seen from the other side.
  • routing.tierName uses its own words (active-tab, recent, priority); in this page they are the answer to "who decides the world".
  • The Developer view still groups the trace by module (formalizer, discourse, router) rather than by step.

How this page is made

The generator replays the owner logs in rules mode (scripts/eval-owner-log.mjs), collects trace.steps of every turn, and counts the edges from the dialogue log (fills), the operations of other worlds and the knowledge catalog reads. The first table (the measures that count) is scripts/landing-table.mjs, computed from the repository, the case corpus (training/eval/cases.jsonl) and the grid. A test (tests/system-map.test.mjs) fails when this page is stale; node scripts/build-system-map.mjs --write regenerates it, and scripts/build-webchat.mjs copies it into the webchat. Libraries (acts, space, session.things, session.dialogue, meta.worlds) are the modules docs/host-boundary.md marks for the sopworld library.
