structure; PSys2 will hand it over when the card type is agreed).
Options: (a) `std.route` with the scorer, cards generated by the library from any loaded world; (b) only the scorer. PSys2 prefers (a): cards from the world's own
`core.word` and `core.pattern` wires need no host.
