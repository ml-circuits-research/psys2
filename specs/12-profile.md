# 12 — The profile world: getting to know the user

World `user.profile` (status `fixture`, English, `formalization=rules`, C2 only, no model). Tab "Profile" (`docs/webchat/ui/tabs/profile.mjs`, plugin `profile`, order 90). Purpose: learn who the user is through a short, natural, game-like conversation, without being annoying, and show what was learned as a CV-like card. Everything runs in the browser; the data stays in this browser (localStorage key `psys2.profile.v1`); nothing is sent anywhere.

## 1. The plan (knowledge base and legend)
- `legend.sop`: type `user.profile.person` whose keys are the profile fields (each key's `ask` is the canonical question), plus the planning types `section`, `field`, `industry`, `reaction`, `activity`.
- `kb.sop` (SOP Lang, each wire with `core.source`): 7 sections (basics, professional, education, skills, interests, goals, preferences) and 21 fields. Each `user.profile.field` has `label`, `section`, `prio` (1 to 10), `card` (`one`/`many`), `sens` (`none`/`low`), `want` (what we would like to learn), `example`, 3 or 4 `q` phrasings (casual, varied), a `follow` question with `followMode` (`always`: asked after a good answer; `unclear`: asked when the answer was not understood) and optional `followKey` (the field the follow-up fills; without it the answer is kept as a note), 3 `ack` templates (`{v}` value, `{V}` capitalised, `{A}` with a/an) and optional `needs` (a field that must be known first).
- Fields: name, address (how to address you), location (city or country), role, field (industry), org, years, previous (roles), education, skills, tools (tools and languages), hobbies, topics, media (books, music, films), sports, learning, career, year (goal for this year), length (short or detailed answers), tone (formal or casual), language (preferred language).
- `industry` wires carry the words that name a field; `reaction` wires hold warm one-line reactions to specific answers ("A nurse: that takes stamina!"); `activity` wires map an activity word to a role (teaching implies teacher).
- **Never asked, never stored unless the user says "remember"**: health, religion, politics, sexuality, exact address, income, age. They are absent from the plan; volunteered, they get a polite "I do not keep things like that unless you say remember" (state `sensitive` only holds what the user asked to keep, shown in the tab and removed by delete). The location field accepts a city or country only.
- `lexicon.sop`: roles per field (`role.<field>`, union `roles`), `fields`, `hobbies`, `music`, `media`, `sports`, `topics`, `interests`, `skills`, `tools`, `subjects`, `langs`, `fieldwords` (names of profile fields for "forget my job"), `styles`, and `sensitive.<category>`. Words ending in `s` also list their lemma (`physics`, `physic`) because pattern slots compare singular lemmas.

## 2. State
`{ fields: { <field>: { v: string | string[], src: 'said' | 'guessed', note?, at } }, skipped: { <field>: 'skip' | 'declined' | 'unclear' | 'forgotten' }, asks, followed, sensitive: [{ cat, text }], pending: null | { kind: 'ask' | 'follow' | 'confirm' | 'delete', key?, ... }, question, active, inputMode: null | 'interview' | 'confirm', plan, counts: { known, total, skipped }, focus: { key, n }, ... }`. `plan` is a compact copy of the knowledge-base plan for the tab (the tab falls back to `PLAN_FALLBACK` of `cv.mjs` before the first operation; a test keeps both equal).

## 3. Dialog policy (the game)
- "interview me", "ask me something", "ask me a question", "get to know me", "let us play get to know me", "start/resume the interview" start it (also off the tab: the world opens the Profile tab). One question at a time: the open field (not known, not skipped, asked fewer than twice, `needs` satisfied, `sens` none or low) with the highest score `prio * 10`, minus 6 for the section just asked and minus 8 for a field already asked once. Phrasing varies with the turn and the number of asks and never repeats the last one; connectors ("Next:", "Now,", "Okay,") vary.
- Each reply: a short warm acknowledgement that reflects the answer (a `reaction` wire for the role, hobby or field, else the field's `ack` template; milestones at 25, 50 and 75 percent), then at most one follow-up for the topic (`followMode=always`, not twice in a row), then the next question with a meter "(6 of 21 known)".
- "skip", "next", "pass", "I don't know", "none" and "I'd rather not say" are respected and remembered (`skipped`, `declined` when the user said they would rather not): the field is never asked again. "pause", "stop", "enough", "not now" end the interview. An answer that is not understood gets one clarifying follow-up; a second failure skips the field silently.
- The interview never asks unprompted: the questions are replies to the user's messages, and the interview stops by itself when the active tab is not Profile (the pending question is asked again on resume).
- **Modal input.** While `inputMode` is set the world's `interceptInput` reads the next message as the answer. It lets pass (returns `handled: false`): this world's own commands (what do you know, forget, delete, export, confirm, ask me about, interview), clear commands and questions for other worlds (open, show, zoom, draw, play, how many, what is, a message ending in `?`, a weak verb such as make or use unless it carries profile content) and long unrelated sentences. A message that says something about the user is read even when it looks like a command.
- Guesses: a role implies a field through the per-field role lexicons. The guess is stored as `src: 'guessed'` and confirmed ("So you work in tech, right?", `inputMode: 'confirm'`): yes makes it `said`, no removes it ("no, healthcare" replaces it). A said value is never overwritten by a guess.

## 4. Understanding (patterns, lexicons, `understand.mjs`)
- `patterns.sop` handle statements that are clear without a pending question: roles with organisation, field, years ("I have been teaching for 12 years"), education, interests (one to three, from the `interests` lexicon), tools, names ("call me Andy"), location, learning goals, answer-style preferences, previous roles, corrections ("no, I am a designer", "actually 10 years"), and the commands (start, pause, what do you know, how much do you know, forget my X, delete my profile, export, confirm, ask me about my X, sensitive volunteering). All are `prof.*` actions; statements become `prof.tell` with the matched slots, which `ops.mjs` rebuilds as a sentence and reads with the same rules as an interview answer. Slots are lexicon-gated (roles, interests, tools, subjects), so off the tab the patterns do not take other worlds' sentences. "call me Andy" names the user on every tab (it never renames the avatar; only "call yourself X" and "your name is X" do): the preferred name is one fact, stored in `user.profile` (`prof.setName`, `prof.nameOf`) and read and written by kids.common ("my name is", "what is my name") through those operations; kids.common keeps its own state only when the profile world is absent. "I live in X" is the profile city on every tab (the noun slot of spec 03 never takes a personal pronoun, so it is no animal fact).
- `understand.mjs` reads free answers: name, role (lexicon, then free text after "I am a"), organisation, field, years (digits and number words, "a decade"), education (level, subject, school), location, hobbies, sports, topics, media, skills, tools, previous roles, goals, answer length, tone, language, addressing; short answers are read as answers to the pending field ("nurse", "12 years", "hiking"); a correction marker (no, actually, sorry, I mean) replaces instead of adding; "forget my job" and "what do you know about me?" are commands, "delete my profile" asks "yes or no" first (`inputMode: 'confirm'`), "delete my profile for good" does not.
- **Plausibility.** Every extracted value is type-checked per field (`fits` in `understand.mjs`): list fields (learning, skills, hobbies, tools ...) take short activity, skill or topic noun phrases, role and organisation short noun phrases (lexicon role or "<noun> at <org>"), years a number, location a short place; a clause or story ("my dad took me": subject-led, finite verb, first-person pronoun) is never a value. A reply that fits no pending field is handled by the interview: if the previous topic's follow-up was asked within the last turns and has no note yet, the reply is stored there as a note ("Thanks, I added that to your hobbies notes."), otherwise it is acknowledged politely and the question is asked again once ("Nice story! And ..."); the second time the normal not-understood path applies. Acknowledgements never echo a value that is not `echoable` (clause-like, long or first-person): they say "Thanks, noted.".
- Export: `prof.export` queues `file.download` (spec 07): JSON (every known field with its source) or a Markdown CV. The tab's buttons send the same commands.

## 5. The tab
A CV-style card: header (initials, name, "role · field", organisation · place · years), a progress ring ("6 of 21 known · 2 skipped"), the pending question while the interview runs, buttons Start/Continue interview, Pause, Skip this, Export JSON, Export CV, Delete (inline confirmation); sections in a grid; each value is a chip marked "said" or "guessed" (with "confirm?" and "not right"); empty fields say what we would like to learn with an example and a "tell me" link; skipped fields say so; private notes; a footer saying that data stays in this browser. Clicks become typed commands through `host.send`. State is restored from and saved to localStorage by the tab; deleting the profile removes the entry.

## 6. Tests and data
`tests.sop` (single statements, sensitive handling, commands, and multi-turn sessions: interview, correction, delete, skip, tab change, export), `tests/profile.test.mjs` (extraction rules, plan consistency, tab registration), and `datagen.mjs` (spec 08): every pattern family, corrections, coordination, other worlds' sentences as negatives.
