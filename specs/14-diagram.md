# 14 — Diagram world: short ids, arrows as objects, arrow geometry (DIAG3)

World `diagram.sketch`, tab `diagram`. The state and the execution are the engine of diagram-vss-demo (`worlds/diagram.sketch/engine/`, never edited by hand); `ops.mjs` wraps it. This spec covers what DIAG3 added on top.

## 1. Short ids
- Every shape has an id by kind: `c` circle, `r` rectangle (box), `s` square, `d` diamond, `e` ellipse, `t` triangle, `h` hexagon, `k` star, `x` text; numbered `c1, c2 ...` from the engine's own per-kind counter (`seq`). Every arrow has `a1, a2 ...` (`edgeSeq`).
- An unnamed shape is *named* by its id (`name == id`). A shape the user named keeps its name and carries the id in `shape.id`.
- Aliases: the id, the old long name (`circle1`, `rectangle1`, `box1`) and the name all resolve to the same shape (`shapeOf`). The engine's `find` is patched to resolve them, and the nodes of a request are canonicalised to names before they run.
- Replies never print ids (RO-PHONE 4): a shape the person did not name is "a circle" / "3 triangles" when drawn, "the circle" (the only one) or "circle 2" afterwards: `Drew a circle.`, `Connected the circle → the rectangle.`, `The arrow Start → Done now says “uses”.` Ids stay valid in what the person types.
- A bare "draw a diagram" / "make a random diagram" draws a random diagram: a diagram kind x a topic whose knowledge has that structure (section 9), never a canned template, and says `I drew a random cycle diagram with four shapes. It is about water cycle.` (the canned `RANDOM_DIAGRAMS` of the first version are deleted).
- The tab draws ids small and grey at the top-left of a shape and on the middle of an arrow; the **Ids** toggle hides them (remembered per browser). Names stay as they are.

## 2. Arrows as objects
An arrow is referred to by its id (`a1`), its ends (`the arrow between c1 and r1`, `the link from A to B`), recency (`the previous / last / that link / arrow / connection / line`, also a bare `label link`), or its label (`the arrow labeled uses`). `refs.mjs` rewrites every form to `the arrow from X to Y`, which the engine's rules reader reads (`from=`, `to=`). Operations: label (a missing text asks `What should the label of a1 (c1 → r1) say?`), colour, style (solid, dashed, dotted), arrowheads (one-way, two-way, none), thickness (`thicker` / `thinner`: one step 1.5x, `a bit` 1.25x, `much` 2x, thinner is the inverse), reverse, delete, select (`select a1`, `highlight the arrow between c1 and r1`: the tab draws a halo; `state.selected = { shapes, edges }`). `thicker and dashed` runs both. Typos of the edge words (`arraw`, `lable`, `conection`) and of long aliases (`reactanle1`) are corrected before reading (`fixTypos`: known words only, one edit, never a name the person gave).

## 3. Arrow geometry (`arrows.mjs`, drawn by the tab)
- An arrow attaches to the side that faces the other end, at least 8 px from the corners of that side (CORNER); circles and ellipses take the point where the line between the centres meets the outline.
- It enters a border at an angle of at least 30 degrees (MIN_ENTRY); otherwise the first and last segments are bent to leave and enter perpendicular (a stub of 14 px).
- Parallel and opposite arrows between the same two shapes are spread 14 px apart (SPREAD).
- A segment keeps 8 px from every other shape (CLEAR); a detour bend is tried, with stubs when the angle needs them. A crowded diagram may leave a smaller gap.
- Labels sit off the line, with a halo in the background colour; the id sits on the line.
- DIAG3b: arrows that meet the same side of a shape (or the same stretch of an ellipse) are fanned out along it, ordered by where the other end lies, at least 10 px apart (APART; 20 px when the side is long enough); a template's trunk is widened to fit its attachment points. Ids drawn on the arrows are kept apart by the same spread.
- DIAG3b: a shape the person just added that lies on an arrow moves to the nearest free spot (clear of other shapes and arrows); an explicit position that is free stays.
- `tests/diagram-arrows.test.mjs` measures the minimum entry angle, the corner clearance and the clearance from other shapes over the 24 templates, the example sessions of the source and crafted fans.

## 4. Sizes and layout by voice
`make c1 a bit bigger / much bigger / the same size as r1`, `make the arrows thicker`, `change / switch the layout to a row / column / grid / circle / tree / layers / radial`, `space them out`, `tidy up`, `untangle`, `straighten the arrows`, `fit` all reach the engine's resize / equalize / arrange / distribute / improve / untangle / straighten / fit. Layout phrases are rewritten to `arrange everything in a X` (`rewriteLayout`).

## 5. Create a diagram and templates
`create / make / start a (new) diagram` with shapes on the canvas asks `Keep the current one or start empty?` and lists the 24 templates (process, cycle, hierarchy, pyramid, funnel, timeline, list, matrix, target, venn, hub, heart, burst, steps, bridge, mountain, spiral, wave, tree, road, balance, hourglass, sunrise, pillars); `keep it` / `start empty` answer it (`diagram.answerNew`, pending state `state.pending`). `clear the canvas` starts empty at once. `a tree-like shape`, `an org chart`, `a flowchart` (read as `process`), `a cycle`, `a pyramid`, `a funnel` build the template. `connect them with a dashed arrow` with no named ends connects the last two shapes drawn.

## 6. Parity with the source
`node scripts/eval-diagram.mjs` also reads every row of `data/test` and `data/bench` with the source rules reader (`imports/diagram-vss-demo/engine/logic.js`, `createSystem().formalize`, loaded by `scripts/lib/diagram-source.mjs`) and prints the parity table (act right, args right, wrong, unread; the table is in `training/RESULTS.md`).

## 7. DIAG3b: templates, ids, old names, selection
- `a tree like shape`, `tree-like`, `tree shape / structure / diagram / chart` mean the **hierarchy** template (the source reader's `tree` is the picture of a tree with roots; a plain `make a tree` keeps it). The reply names the template (`Drew a hierarchy of ... as the group Hierarchy1.`).
- Any command other than the answer to `Keep the current one or start empty?` answers it as keep: the reply starts with `Keeping the current diagram.` and a later `start empty` finds nothing to confirm.
- Shapes made by a template get short ids like any shape (`r1`, `e1`); replies never show a part name (`Tree1-trunk`, `Branch1`).
- `rectangle1`, `circle2` ... mean the Nth shape of that kind the person drew; shapes made by a template do not count (their ids follow the same numbering).
- The state exposes `kind` (circle, rectangle ...) of every shape.
- Only a selection marks shapes: `select ...`, or the answer of a question; a template or a command that makes shapes marks nothing.
- Spelling: among equally close candidates the one made of the same letters wins (`lable` is `label`, not `able`).
- "in the diagram" / "on the diagram" after a shape request (`pat.diagram.sketch.create.in`, `signal=true`) names this world: "draw three circles in the diagram" is a diagram request on the Drawing tab too, not a place for the drawing world.

## 8. Diagram shaped like a thing (DRAW-FROM-FACTS)
"A diagram shaped like a house", "make a diagram shaped like a lighthouse", "arrange the boxes as a house" (`pat.diagram.sketch.shaped`, `.shaped.arrange`, action `diagram.shapedLike`, `shapedLike` in `ops.mjs`). The thing is an open slot read after the pattern: it is looked up in the catalog of drawable things (`worlds/art.draw/things.catalog.mjs`, from `knowledge/k.objects`, spec 07 section 7). One rectangle is created per part of the object, in draw order, named after the part (`left_window`; a name that is taken gets a number) and labelled with the part name ("left window"); its position and size come from the part's box `x y w h` in the object's 0-100 frame, scaled uniformly so that the whole fits the canvas (about 700 x 410) and centred. Nothing else is created: no arrows, no extra boxes; the colours of the parts are not copied (the diagram's own defaults apply). One undo takes the whole diagram back. A layout word in the slot (row, column, grid, circle, tree ...) with shapes on the canvas is the engine's own arrange, so the existing layouts do not change; a thing with no facts is answered plainly: "I don't know how to draw a diagram shaped like a blorp yet." and nothing is created.

**Nodes on an outline do not overlap (reviewer 2026-11-15 04:50).** The current arrangement is along the outline (spec 16 section 4, `outlineOf`, `pointsAlong`). The points along it come from the library's `arrange(nodes, 'path', { points, closed: true })` (sopworld 0.129). Every arrangement along an outline goes through `fitOnOutline(points)` (`engine.mjs`): the nodes get the engine's usual size (62 px) when the shortest gap between neighbouring points leaves a 10 px margin; otherwise one size for all, that gap less the margin (at least 24 px); corners closer than the smallest node plus the margin are one node at their middle. The house's eaves stay (7 nodes of 28 px on the 800 x 500 canvas: an eave is 38 px from its wall top). Shapes the person drew that are moved onto an outline keep their places evenly spaced, and a shape bigger than the spacing is resized to fit. Test: `tests/draw-from-facts.test.mjs` (no two nodes overlap; 14 circles on a house; the rule itself).

**The outline comes from the catalog (Q21, 2026-11-15).** The 50 outlines of k.layouts (17 shapes of k.shapes, 33 drawings traced; points in a 0-100 frame, clockwise from the top) are bundled from the pin with the w.lang.en frames of the shard shape-frames (`src/knowledge/bundled-diagrams.mjs`, `outlines` and `shapeFrames`). A sentence asks for an arrangement when one of those frames reads it (`outlineIn` of `src/knowledge/diagram-world.mjs`: the frame's words before its role `arrangement`, an optional article, an item of the lexicon outline.shape or outline.shaped, the words after it): "in the shape of a house", "shaped like a star", "house-shaped" ("-shaped" stripped), "in heart form", "arrange the boxes in a star shape"; the frame's words with a thing that is no outline ("shaped like a blorp", "in xyzzy form") get "I don't know how to draw a diagram shaped like a blorp yet. The catalog has no outline of a blorp." The host's silhouette tracer (a grid of the drawing's mass, its boundary, Douglas-Peucker) is deleted. What stays host geometry is small: a traced outline's points go to the drawing's own corner within the outline's `tolerance` (the exact gable top and eaves), the corners of a one-sided small detail of the drawing (a filled part under 8 % of the largest with no mirror twin: the chimney) are left out, and corners whose triangle is under 1 % of the area go. The house is the same 7 nodes as before.

**Pronouns (2026-11-14).** The engine reader takes `ctx.last` as the array of names the last command made or named (`state.last`): one name is "it", several are "them". `engine.mjs` `readerCtx` passes that array (a `{ref}` object, as it did before, was read as no referent, so "make it red" failed with "Which shape should I change?" even across turns and in a clause after "add a circle and"). With it, "add a circle and colour it blue" and "add a circle then make it red" work through the shared clauses-in-sequence reading (spec 03). The other executor worlds (art.draw, docs.editor, data.charts, plan.seating) resolve a pronoun from the focus of the shared executor; geo.map and media.music have no creatable things named by a pronoun. Tests: `tests/clauses-in-sequence.test.mjs`.

## 9. Diagrams composed from knowledge (sopworld 0.112 `composeDiagram` / `sampleDiagram`; knowledge `k.diagrams`, `w.diagram`; 2026-11-15)
**Rule.** A diagram of a kind is not a template in a world. The kinds are knowledge (`k.diagrams.kind`: flowchart, tree, cycle, timeline, mind map, Venn diagram, org chart, network, pipeline, comparison table), each with the shapes of its roles (`k.diagrams.shape`) and the **fills** that say which knowledge has its structure (`k.diagrams.fill kind= type= key= gives=edges|order|members|values topic=`: the parts of a thing, the kinds under a kind, the steps of a method, the stages of a cycle, a country and its cities or neighbours). The library composes a kind x a topic into `w.diagram` wires laid out by `arrange`; this world shows them as shapes and arrows. No kind word, topic, label or sample is written in this repository.
**Code.** `src/knowledge/diagram-world.mjs` (host side, browser-safe), `worlds/diagram.sketch/engine.mjs` (`composedDiagram`, `diagramSentences`), the hook `diagramGoal` in `own.mjs` (loads the live catalog's knowledge for the request's words before the world runs, like `planGoal`).
**The request.** `parseDiagramRequest` reads "[verb] a/the <kind> [diagram|chart] of|about|for|on <topic>" (the kind is a name or form of a kind of the knowledge, one or two words, with or without "diagram"), "<topic> on|as|in a <kind>" (a weak reading: with no knowledge it is left to the engine), and the bare random forms ("make a random diagram", "draw a diagram", "a sample diagram", "a random mind map"). A kind alone ("draw a tree", "make a cycle") is not a request: the engine draws the shape or the template as before. A sentence with no verb ("a mind map about honey bees") is read when the open tab's reader hands it to the diagram. **The topic** is tried as said and as windows of its words (longest first, later first; singular, and the base of an -ing word: "making tea" -> "make tea"; never beginning or ending in a function word) until the knowledge has the structure.
**Honesty.** A candidate is accepted only when what the fill read is about the topic exactly: a fill looked up by a topic key has a fact whose value of that key is the topic, a fill with no key has a fact named the topic; a fill with no topic key that holds for any topic (the elements of a story, the aspects of a thing) is never an answer to a named topic (the library takes it; the host does not). When several fills of the kind have the topic, the one whose type, key or `by=` names a word of the request ("parts" -> `k.parts.usual`, "cities" -> `k.geo.city`, "kinds" -> `k.kinds.parent`, "by distance" -> the fill `by=distance`) is drawn, and among equals the first in the knowledge. Every fill of the kind is tried (each through a view of the World in which the kind has only that fill), so a request draws one diagram whatever the seed and the reasoner; the seed only places a free layout (2026-11-15: "a timeline of the planets by distance" had been drawn by size on some seeds, which read as a matrix fault). **Not for a person.** A fact that says `user_facing=false` (the drawing methods of PSys2 in `k.procedures.method`), and a topic that names one through the fill's topic key (its slot's `type=... by=`), is never drawn for a named topic, never sampled and never offered by the dice; the bundled subset carries those facts and the schemas of their types where the facts have the key. With none the reply says so (`I do not know enough about X to draw a tree of it yet.`) and nothing is drawn; a list of labels ("a flowchart of Start, Process and End") and a weak reading are the engine's own as before.
**The picture.** One `create` per node (named after its label, made unique; shape from `k.diagrams.shape`: oval -> ellipse, rectangle and its variants -> rectangle, circle, diamond, chevron -> hexagon, dot -> ellipse), one `connect` per edge (an arrowhead when the kind's edges are `directed`, a plain line when `undirected`, none for `none`; a kind with `edges=none` whose structure is `sequence`, the timeline, is drawn on its line: plain segments join its events in order), a Venn set is an ellipse drawn first round its members. The layout is the library's; the canvas shows the whole extent of the nodes (a layer wider than the frame is laid out wider, not squeezed), so the boxes do not overlap. The diagram is one undo step. With shapes on the canvas it is drawn on top of them and says so (`It is on top of what was there; undo takes it away.`): starting a new diagram stays the question of section 5. A composed diagram has its own layout, so the engine does not move its shapes aside (`placeFree`).
**Random.** `sampleDiagram` (kind x topic, seeded; the walk over seeds keeps the first with at least four shapes and a topic its knowledge is about, at most nine nodes). The reply says the kind and the topic. The dice of the Diagram tab (spec 25) says whole sentences of the same kind x topic (`sampleKnowledge` list `diagrams`, "Make a mind map of bee.", from `diagramTopics`, kept when they compose and read back).
**Knowledge.** The bundled subset (`src/knowledge/bundled-diagrams.mjs`, generated by `scripts/build-bundled-diagrams.mjs <catalog docs/worlds>` from knowledge `ebb2aa5`): `k.diagrams` and the `w.diagram` schema whole, and the wires of the types the fills name that are small enough (a type over 90 kB is left to the catalog): the usual parts, the steps of methods, the cycles of nature and the calendar (`k.time`), a story's elements, a genre's parts, the aspects of a thing, the joins of `k.layouts`; the countries come from the composer's bundled `k.geo`. The live catalog is added over it (`diagramHostOf(cat)`: the kinds and fills first, which name the worlds, then a small world whole and a big one by the shards whose keys name a word of the request), so a tree of kinds, a Venn diagram of kinds and the parts of things the subset lacks need the catalog; offline they say they do not know.
**Measured** (`scripts/measure-diagram-gap.mjs`, 60 requests judged by state): see `docs/lessons.md` and `worker-log.md` (2026-11-15): arrangements 14/20 (the six rows lost to the avatar world are arbitration, Q15 (3)), kinds 3 -> 9 of 18, edits 11/12, random 10 canned templates -> 10 diagrams of a kind about a topic. The kinds rows left are knowledge or reading items, ledgered by cause (`training/eval/failures.jsonl`, source `DIAG-KNOW`).
**Tests.** `tests/diagram-knowledge.test.mjs` (request reading, candidates, composition, honesty, the picture, random, the dice, the live route with a fake catalog, sessions); the world's own `tests.sop` (random rows read "I drew a random ").
