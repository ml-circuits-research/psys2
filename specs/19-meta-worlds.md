# 19 — meta.worlds: choosing the world by rules with reasons (WORLD-OF-WORLDS)

Implementation: `worlds/meta.worlds/` (legend, generated cards, rules), `src/dialog/meta-worlds.mjs`, `src/dialog/symbolic-router.mjs`, `scripts/build-world-cards.mjs`, experiment `scripts/eval-world-routing.mjs` and `training/psys2_train/world_scores.py`. Tests: `tests/meta-worlds.test.mjs`. Results: `training/results/world-routing-2026-11-13.json` and the entry in `worker-log.md`.

## Purpose
Choosing the right tab is a symbolic problem. `meta.worlds` is a shared module (a world with no tab, no patterns, no operations; never a routing candidate) that knows what each world is and picks the world for a message from what each world is and from the live state. A new world is added by its generated card alone, with no retraining of the dispatcher's world head.

## The three parts
1. **Cards** (`kb.sop`, generated by `scripts/build-world-cards.mjs`, never edited; `tests/meta-worlds.test.mjs` fails when the file is stale). One `meta.worlds.card` per world that has patterns: `id`, `tab`, `kind` (tab or module), `run=needs` (a game: nothing to take until it runs), `nav=yes` (the world that opens tabs), `name` (aliases of the tab, the last part of the world id), `act` (action words: the mandatory literal words and phrase alternatives of the world's patterns, plus the words of the canonical examples of two patterns or more; a word belongs to the world that has at least half of all its uses, `actshared` when it has a fifth), `thing` (the words of its thing and noun lexicons; `thingshared` when three or four worlds have the word, left out at five), `frame` (the first word pairs of the canonical questions), `catalog=yes` (art.draw and talk take any thing of the knowledge catalog). Words are lemmas. Size gate: a card is at most 40 lines and all cards are under 5% of the patterns' bytes.
2. **Live facts** (`liveFromContext`, cheap, from the state the host has): the open tab's world, the worlds with something running (an active game, an open modal), the world that asked a question last turn (`discourse.pending`), the world of the last action (`discourse.frames[0]`), the recent worlds, and the names and kinds of the things in every scene (`sceneThings`). Plus the message: its words, pairs and triples, whether it is a piece without a verb of its own, the words that are things of the catalog.
3. **Rules** (`matrix.sop`, SOP Lang, `core.rule` with `not=`). Each rule derives `meta.worlds.vote` wires (world, rule, points, the word it rests on) or `meta.worlds.block` wires (world, rule). The host adds the points per world, drops the blocked worlds and says each reason from the `meta.worlds.say` table (plain words, e.g. "the crocodile is in your drawing"). The reasons go to the trace (`decision.reasons`, `decision.reason` = "because ...").

| rule | points | when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `named` | 100 | the message names the world ("the chess board") |
| `nav` | 130 | a navigation word of the world that opens tabs while another world is named ("open the chess tab") |
| `asked` / `askedany` | 90 / 25 | the world asked a question last turn, and the message is a piece / anything |
| `followup` | 80 | a piece without a verb of its own, and the world of the last action |
| `fragrun`, `fragtab` | 60, 50 | a piece, and a game is running / the world's tab is open |
| `referent` | 70 | a thing the message names is in the world's scene |
| `actthing` | 45 | an action word and a kind of thing of the same world |
| `act`, `thing` | 25 | one of them alone |
| `actshared`, `thingshared` | 10, 6 | the word is shared by three or four worlds |
| `frame` | 30 | a question frame of the world |
| `catalog` | 8 | a thing of the knowledge catalog, for a world that takes any |
| `active`, `recent` | 12, 8 | the open tab; a world that answered lately |
| `encoder` | up to 30 | the encoder's probability times its weight (a prior; the only signal that is not a rule) |
| block `notrunning` | | the world needs something running, nothing runs, it is not the open tab, a tab is open, the message does not start anything and does not name it |
| block `nocontinuation` | | the message is a piece without a verb of its own, something was said before, a tab is open, and the world is not the last, the open tab, running, asked, anywhere (shell, help, names), named or holding a referent of the piece (SCENE-NATURAL P1) |

A block is not a vote: nothing outvotes it, the encoder included. Caps keep a long message from winning on repetition (two votes per rule for action, thing and referent). `decide` asks (no world) when the best world has fewer than 15 points or the best two are within 4 points.

SCENE-NATURAL P1 lives here: `foreignFragment` (src/dialog/continuation.mjs) keeps only the structural test of the reading (no verb of its own, a pronoun with a scene) and asks `meta.worlds` whether the world is blocked; the no-continuation veto of `world-aware.mjs` is unchanged.

## The setting `routing`
`settings.routing` (not in the shared runtime defaults until the reviewer decides): `dispatcher` (A, default: the encoder proposes worlds, the rules validate), `symbolic` (B: `meta.worlds` replaces the world proposals of the dispatcher's prediction, and orders the candidate readings of the rule formalizer; the encoder's world head is not read) and `symbolic+encoder` (C: as B, the encoder's probabilities are the prior). The host awaits `engine.prepareMeta()` once. Seams: `rerankPrediction` (encoder-dispatch) and `createSymbolicRouter` (the router interface of ADR-004, used by `session._router`). What a world reads is not changed.

## The experiment
`scripts/eval-world-routing.mjs`: for each message of the operational set (single-world messages), the dialogues (each turn, the session replayed), the minimal pairs, the knowledge-lookup set, the owner's log (messages with a world expectation) and a seeded wrong-tab set (160 canonical requests of every world said on another tab, 40 fragments after a turn said on another tab; plus 200 operational messages on another tab), the world each router picks is compared with the world of the case: right, wrong, or asked (no world taken), and the time. The encoder's world distributions come from `psys2_train.world_scores` (CUDA, bf16, one batch). The hold-out masks one world's encoder probability (renormalised) without retraining and routes with B and C. Tables in `worker-log.md`.
