# 20 — meta.worlds: choosing the world by rules with reasons (WORLD-OF-WORLDS)

Implementation: `worlds/meta.worlds/` (legend, generated cards, rules), `src/dialog/meta-worlds.mjs`, `src/dialog/symbolic-router.mjs`, `scripts/build-world-cards.mjs`, experiment `scripts/eval-world-routing.mjs` and `training/psys2_train/world_scores.py`. Tests: `tests/meta-worlds.test.mjs`. Results: `training/results/world-routing-2026-11-13.json` and the entry in `worker-log.md`.

## Purpose
Choosing the right tab is a symbolic problem. `meta.worlds` is a shared module (a world with no tab, no patterns, no operations; never a routing candidate) that knows what each world is and picks the world for a message from what each world is and from the live state. A new world is added by its generated card alone, with no retraining of the dispatcher's world head.

## The three parts
1. **Cards** (`kb.sop`, generated by `scripts/build-world-cards.mjs`, never edited; `tests/meta-worlds.test.mjs` fails when the file is stale). One `meta.worlds.card` per world that has patterns: `id`, `tab`, `kind` (tab or module), `run=needs` (a game: nothing to take until it runs), `nav=yes` (the world that opens tabs), `name` (aliases of the tab, the last part of the world id), `act` (action words: the mandatory literal words and phrase alternatives of the world's patterns, plus the words of the canonical examples of two patterns or more; a word belongs to the world that has at least half of all its uses, `actshared` when it has a fifth), `thing` (the words of its thing and noun lexicons; `thingshared` when three or four worlds have the word, left out at five), `frame` (the first word pairs of the canonical questions), `catalog=yes` (art.draw and talk take any thing of the knowledge catalog). Words are lemmas. Size gate: a card is at most 40 lines and all cards are under 5% of the patterns' bytes.
2. **Live facts** (`liveFromContext`, cheap, from the state the host has): the open tab's world, the worlds with something running (an active game, an open modal), the world that asked a question last turn (`discourse.pending`), the world of the last action (`discourse.frames[0]`), the recent worlds, and the names and kinds of the things in every scene (`sceneThings`). Plus the message: its words, pairs and triples, whether it is a piece without a verb of its own, the words that are things of the catalog.
3. **Rules** (`matrix.sop`, SOP Lang, `core.rule` with `not=`). Each rule derives `meta.worlds.vote` wires (world, rule, points, the word it rests on) or `meta.worlds.block` wires (world, rule). The host adds the points per world, drops the blocked worlds and says each reason from the `meta.worlds.say` table (plain words, e.g. "the crocodile is in your drawing"). The reasons go to the trace (`decision.reasons`, `decision.reason` = "because ...").

| rule | points | when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `named` | 100 | the message names the world ("the chess board") |
| `nav` | 130 | a navigation word of the world that opens tabs while another world is named ("open the chess tab") |
| `asked` / `askedany` | 90 / 25 | the world asked a question last turn, and the message is a piece / anything |
| `followup` | 80 | a piece without a verb of its own, and the world of the last action |
| `fragrun`, `fragtab` | 60, 50 | a piece, and a game is running / the world's tab is open |
| `referent` | 70 | a thing the message names is in the world's scene |
| `actthing` | 45 | an action word and a kind of thing of the same world |
| `act`, `thing` | 25 | one of them alone |
| `actshared`, `thingshared` | 10, 6 | the word is shared by three or four worlds |
| `frame` | 30 | a question frame of the world |
| `catalog` | 8 | a thing of the knowledge catalog, for a world that takes any |
| `active`, `recent` | 12, 8 | the open tab; a world that answered lately |
| `encoder` | up to 30 | the encoder's probability times its weight (a prior; the only signal that is not a rule) |
| block `notrunning` | | the world needs something running, nothing runs, it is not the open tab, a tab is open, the message does not start anything and does not name it |
| block `nocontinuation` | | the message is a piece without a verb of its own, something was said before, a tab is open, and the world is not the last, the open tab, running, asked, anywhere (shell, help, names), named or holding a referent of the piece (SCENE-NATURAL P1) |

A block is not a vote: nothing outvotes it, the encoder included. Caps keep a long message from winning on repetition (two votes per rule for action, thing and referent). `decide` asks (no world) when the best world has fewer than 15 points or the best two are within 4 points.

SCENE-NATURAL P1 lives here: `foreignFragment` (src/dialog/continuation.mjs) keeps only the structural test of the reading (no verb of its own, a pronoun with a scene) and asks `meta.worlds` whether the world is blocked; the no-continuation veto of `world-aware.mjs` is unchanged.

## Second round (2026-11-13 evening)
- **Cards also from** `describe.sop` (verbs, examples), `operations.sop` (operation names), the questions of `legend.sop`, the inputs of `tests.sop` (a word of two inputs or more counts as an action word), `trigger` lexicons and the optional phrase groups the world made for itself; `slot=number` / `slot=agent` when at least a fifth of its patterns take a number or a person's name; a world's tab id or last id part is its `name` when other worlds' patterns use the word at most twice ("hangman", "chess"; not "draw").
- **Lexicon index** (derived when the module loads from the worlds' lexicon declarations, never stored; no size problem for geo.map or avatar): a word of one or two worlds' lexicons is `lexword` (25), of three or four `lexshared` (6); words already on the card are not counted twice. Names (`name` lexicons) set the `agent` slot word, digits and number words the `number` slot word (`slotagent` 30, `slotnumber` 6).
- **Near words**: a word of a message that is not on a card is read as the card word one slip away (insertion, deletion, substitution, swap; `slipDistance` of the scene referents; two slips from 8 letters), or its stem (plural/case endings, -ing, -ed).
- **Rules**: `nav` is not given when the named world is already the open tab ("let's play chess" on the chess tab); `active` 24 points (was 12).
- **Confidence-aware prior** (`route`, `DEFAULT_PRIOR` = `{ max: 60, min: 8, span: 45, conf: 0 }`): the rules are derived without the prior first; the prior weighs `max` when no world leads and shrinks linearly to `min` when the best world leads the second by `span` points; `conf` (default 0) drops the prior when the encoder's best probability is below it. A world the encoder has no label for has no probability (absent, the others are not renormalised).
- **Refusal as evidence**: `live.refused` (worlds whose dry run says they would refuse the message in the present state) blocks them (`refused`). The host that computes it is not wired yet (sopworld 0.16.0 Round 19 is not vendored; the `notrunning` rule for games stays until it is).
- **Default**: `routing` is `symbolic+encoder` (C) when the setting is missing; A (`dispatcher`) is selectable in Settings > Developer and About says which router is on.

## The setting `routing`
`settings.routing`: `dispatcher` (A: the encoder proposes worlds, the rules validate), `symbolic` (B: `meta.worlds` replaces the world proposals of the dispatcher's prediction, and orders the candidate readings of the rule formalizer; the encoder's world head is not read) and `symbolic+encoder` (C, the default: as B, the encoder's probabilities are the prior). The host awaits `engine.prepareMeta()` once. Seams: `rerankPrediction` (encoder-dispatch) and `createSymbolicRouter` (the router interface of ADR-004, used by `session._router`). What a world reads is not changed. **C with the encoder, B as the documented fallback** (model ablation 2026-11-13: C 3,240 against B 2,814 right of 3,984 at world level; end to end the three routers are within 3 messages once the rules validate): while the model loads, is missing, failed or is paused, the same setting `symbolic+encoder` runs as B (no prior is available, `meta.worlds` alone chooses); nothing is switched, the prior simply is absent. The webchat says so in PROGRESS (note under the bar while the model loads, and the failure line).

## The experiment
`scripts/eval-world-routing.mjs`: for each message of the operational set (single-world messages), the dialogues (each turn, the session replayed), the minimal pairs, the knowledge-lookup set, the owner's log (messages with a world expectation) and a seeded wrong-tab set (160 canonical requests of every world said on another tab, 40 fragments after a turn said on another tab; plus 200 operational messages on another tab), the world each router picks is compared with the world of the case: right, wrong, or asked (no world taken), and the time. The encoder's world distributions come from `psys2_train.world_scores` (CUDA, bf16, one batch). The hold-out masks one world's encoder probability (renormalised) without retraining and routes with B and C. Tables in `worker-log.md`.

## Arbitration of generated worlds (2026-11-14)
Where the choice is made in the formalizer's decline step (`src/formalizer/rules/index.mjs`, `rankMatches`), as rules for every world with an `accepts` hook (spec 21), never as entries per phrase:
1. **The open tab owns its acts first.** `accepts` may return `'focus'` (the world holds the dialogue: its reading does not yield to a reading without free text); the acts executor passes the verdict through. A world that declines an act `remove` in the open tab, or says `'refuse'` (the reading is its own but names a thing it has not: "draw a unicorn" in the drawing tab), keeps the reading as a plain refusal ("I can't find the unicorn in the picture") unless another world reads the sentence. Another world's free-text reading of a bare act (at most two literal words, no list or frame word of its own) is claimed only when every word of its free slots occurs in that world's own state (rule 2, open slots resolve).
2. **A world claims only a reading the sentence fully supports.** The acts executor declines a role phrase that coordinates things when any conjunct is unsupported ("clear everything and zork the plimp": the clause is left unread and the leniency stage reads the clauses one by one); a bare form whose modifiers start with a value verb and "to" is that act's sentence ("change vatar to a man"); the drawing board outside its tab claims only things whose every word it reads, lists ("show X") only of things, colours or classes it knows ("show Pariss" is the map's), and "with" only followed by words it reads. A lexicon word that the singular lemma would cut ("canvas") also matches in its own form.
3. **Refusal is plain in a tab without the thing.** See 1.
4. **No reading changes the tab unless a world is named.** A reading of a message that has a clause nobody read (or a corrected, ignored or best-effort reading) brings no tab forward, including the world's own `ui.openTab` request (`session.noTabFollow`).
5. **A referent found in another world's things is evidence for that world (2026-11-14).** When the open tab's reading of an act that needs a thing that is there ("remove X") names words that are not in the tab world's own state, and every one of them is a whole word of the state of a world the user worked in lately (`recentWorlds`: a shopping list that holds milk), the tab's reading is dropped (no refusal, no `ownDeclined` restore) and the sentence is read by the world that holds the evidence ("remove milk" on the drawing board: "I took that as: remove milk from my list"). The tab does not change (rule 4), a thing that IS in the tab's picture stays with the tab, and nothing is listed per phrase: the evidence is the state, the same lookup (`freeWordsResolve`'s whole-word match) as rule 2. Worlds never used in the session are no evidence (sample content such as the reader's stories).
