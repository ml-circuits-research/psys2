# 24. Plans from knowledge

Complex and planned actions come from knowledge and orchestration, not from rules inside live worlds (owner, 2026-11-14). A request that is a **goal** ("draw a farm", "a person on the swing", "a family photo", "a doctor", "set a table for four", "put a hat on the man") is reached by **methods**: facts of the catalog world `k.procedures` (what a scene has, which anchor holds a kind, what a role wears, the slots of a layout, the steps of a named task). The planner is the library's `std.plan` (sopworld 0.55: `planner`, `factStore`, `PlanLive`; one copy, never written again here). A live world only **executes** the acts of a plan.

## Flow

1. **Reading.** The kinds lexicon of a world that executes plans is open to the goal words of the catalog (`catalog=k.people/shards/roles`, `k.scenes/shards/scenes`, `k.procedures/shards/tasks` in `schema.sop`: a catalog entry is a world or a shard prefix of one, `src/knowledge/open-words.mjs`). "farm", "doctor" and "family photo" are things the board reads, so arbitration lets the board have the sentence in its tab.
2. **Goal.** `worlds/art.draw/plan.mjs` `goalOf` turns the roles of the reading (or, when the reader split the sentence otherwise, its own words, `goalFromText`) into `{ act, roles }` in the planner's terms (`kind`, `number`, `place: { value, rel }`, `material`). A kind is said as the knowledge names it (`host.canonical`: the `core.word` wires of `k.procedures.goal.value`: "group photo" is "family photo"). A place with no relation word of the planner (`on in around`) is not a goal: the board reads it itself. "With" is the exception for a kind that is itself a goal ("a queen with a crown"): the goal is the kind and `also` the thing it should have, planned on the first thing only when the first plan did not make it (one undo step, and said when it cannot be done). A thing of the catalog that is not drawn yet, put somewhere ("put a scarf on the girl"), is the goal of making it there, over the thing that is drawn (the planner binds the live things).
3. **Loading** (`src/plan/host.mjs`, lazy: nothing at boot, nothing for "draw a house"). The first reading that may be a goal (a kind the board has no picture of, a thing with `on` / `in` / `around`, a task word) loads `k.procedures`, `k.layouts`, `k.wearables`, `k.people`, `k.feelings`, the `acts` shard of `k.actions`, the schema of `k.kinds`, and of the big worlds only the shards the goal's words name (`k.scenes`: the shards whose index keys hold the word). The kinds the plan makes are asked of the catalog in the same turn (`cat.thing`, at most 30), so the board runs the plan with everything at hand. A catalog that cannot be reached loads nothing: the board's own reading goes on, as before.
4. **Plan.** `planner(store, { chain }).plan(goal, live)` over the things of the picture. No method fits: the board's own path. A needed role missing: the board's own question.
5. **Execution** (`plan.mjs` `runPlan`): `make` is the board's add (a kind it cannot draw but the people's `drawing` names is drawn as that: a doctor is a person); a place on an anchor or a wearable place puts the thing in the box that `k.layouts` / `k.wearables` state, in the frame of the target thing (resting on the anchor's line, or centred and scaled in a box); `around` is a ring; a layout group is the rows of its slots, then one group thing. Nothing here names a kind, scene, role, anchor or slot.
6. **One request, one undo step** (specs/15 "one step per request": the plan is ONE request, so it is one step; specs/21 section 9 would also allow one step per act group, and a plan is exactly an act group). `engine.mjs` `oneStep` restores the picture when the plan does nothing and collapses the steps of the plan into the first. A step the board cannot do (a kind it cannot draw, a dependent step whose target is not there) is **left out and said** ("I left out a plate (I cannot draw it yet)"); when nothing at all can be done the answer is a refusal and the picture is as it was.
7. **Words.** The reply names the steps in plain words (what was made, with numbers; what was put where; what was left out). "Why?" after a plan names the method and the methods inside it, then the steps (`discourse._because` asks the world's `whyPlan` hook, only while the plan is the newest change).

## What is measured

`scripts/plans-set.mjs` (60 requests: scenes 14, roles and wearables 9, anchors 14, layouts and tasks 8, take apart / build / group 7, documents / charts / transfers 8) judged by the state of the live worlds (objects made, geometry on the anchor, the reply not a refusal), `scripts/eval-plans.mjs --root <checkout>` on any checkout, with the live catalog. Before the plans: 19 of 60; with them: 51 of 60, and 56 of 60 after the ledger by cause (docs/lessons.md "plans ledger by cause"; both reasoners); right to wrong: 0. The 4 left are catalog asks (a role on an anchor, the parts of a whole: `handover/knowledge-out/catalog-lacks.md`) and transfers of the picture's things into another world. `tests/plans.test.mjs` runs the host, the executor, one undo step and all-or-nothing on a toy catalog offline, and the set when the catalog is at hand (`PSYS2_KNOWLEDGE`). `scripts/test-webchat-plans.mjs` runs it in Chrome.

## Not planned here (and why)

- **Take apart, build from parts**: the hand path cuts the real geometry of the drawing (`takeApart`); the plan has no coordinates for the parts of a drawing. Kept.
- **A chart / a document about the discussion**: `k.procedures` makes a chart in `w.list` and a report in `w.timeline`, worlds the live set does not have as schemas; the hand path (session.things, `things.show`) carries the topics, games and places of the session. Kept.
- **The random surprise scene** (`D.scene`) is the offline fallback of "compose a scene" until the plan worlds have a bundled subset.
