# 26. Summary and composition (host side of `std.summarize` and `std.compose`)

Status: `std.summarize` (0.61) and a first `std.compose` (0.64/0.65) are released and wired. The composition runs over the bundled catalog subset (k.interest, k.genres, k.animals, k.geo). The five document types map to the published `k.genres` genres, but **sopworld 0.65 reads `move=` on a genre and the published genres have parts and steps**, so it throws on them: only the essay is composed (by the library's default moves, the genre named as not applied); the other four types are declined with that reason until the library reads parts and steps.

## Rule
No LLM, no local generator, no fallback text. Summaries and compositions come from `std.summarize` / `std.compose` through `src/dialog/textgen.mjs`. Without them the adapters are `available: false`, the UI that needs them is not shown (the Talk paragraph, the Documents buttons and form), and the commands decline plainly ("I cannot write compositions yet: the library has no composer (std.compose) in this build.").

## Interface (as implied by the reviewer's block of 2026-11-14 14:45; to be confirmed with the library)
- **Real `std.summarize` (0.61):** `summarizeLog(entries, { parentOf, length })` takes `[{ act, kind, failed?, changed? }]` and returns FRAMES `[{ act, kind, count, kinds?, failed?, salience }]` (aggregated by act and kind, siblings generalised through `parentOf`; `length` line / paragraph = 3 / bullets = all). `summarizeText(text, { n, stop, redundancy })` returns `{ sentences: [{ text, score, at }], picked: [text] }` (extractive; language-free). The library writes no English.
- **Host rendering (TEMPORARY, until the language world renders frames):** `renderFrames` in `src/dialog/textgen.mjs` turns frames into "You drew two trees and a house; one request was not understood or could not be done." Entries come from what the worlds EXECUTED (`executedActs` in `src/dialog/conversation.mjs`): per supported answer, the operation that ran (`op.art.draw.make` gives world and act) and, for each thing it added or changed, the kind the world's own state records. Turns that were refused or not understood have no entries and are only counted; the user's words never reach the paragraph. Taught facts are narrated only when the knowledge world stored a relation in English (`narratable`: `word is_a kind`, `your kind is called name`); other stored facts (free attributes of an unknown word) are not narrated. Failed frames carry a salience bonus, so the host asks for all frames (`bullets`), counts the failures in the sentence and shows the best three that worked. The paragraph has at most three sentences: what was done (frames), the worlds used (every one, by id), what was taught (every fact). `parentOf` is not passed yet (no generalisation of siblings; a follow-up from the knowledge catalog).
- **Real `std.compose` (0.71, pinned 0.72):** `compose(world, topic, { genre, size: { sentences }, seed, realise })` over a World holding the knowledge. With a published `k.genres` genre the library lays the text out by the genre: `{ genre, register, topic, seed, parts: [{ part, heading?, relation?, moves: [frame] }], story?, short? }`; a frame is `{ part, move, takes, tense, cites | recalls | element+value, fact?, text?, template? }`. The genre decides the parts and their repeats (the solver picks them), `size.sentences` is a budget (the chapters and subchapters of a request do not apply to a genre). Without a `realise` option the library realises the frames through the discourse templates of `w.lang.en` (`discourseRealiser`: a template of the frame's move and register whose slots all fill; no sentence twice while another fits); a frame with no fitting template has no text and is listed in `short`. Stories are told from a plan of `k.story` (`readStory`, `storyPlan`). Every frame that states a claim cites its wire; a composition is deterministic by its seed.
- **Host mapping (`createComposer` in `src/dialog/textgen.mjs`, `src/dialog/compose-world.mjs`, `src/lang/en-generate.mjs`):** the World is the bundled catalog subset (`knowledgeWorld`: k.genres, k.interest, k.animals, k.geo, and `src/knowledge/bundled-compose.mjs`, generated by `scripts/build-bundled-compose.mjs`: k.story, w.lang.en's discourse and act templates, the irregular plurals, auxiliaries and prepositions, k.scenes, k.actions, k.people, the names of k.objects, what k.commonsense, k.attributes and k.parts say about the animals). Type -> `genre` when the world holds `k.genres.genre name=<type>`. The words asked set the sentence budget (`words / 16`). The sentences are the library's: the host passes only what the language owns (`englishOf(lib, world).realiser(seed)`: `np`, `clause`, `inflect`, and a `fill` that says a fact only when it is about the topic); there is no host template and no host frame renderer for composition. A text that claims nothing (no sentence citing a wire) is declined: "I know nothing about X in my knowledge yet". The result is memoised by (type, topic, size, seed) in the composer, and `docr.compose` derives its seed from the session and the request, so the engine's dry run and real run write the same document. Chapters of the document are the parts that say something.
- `checkComposition` (frames): at least one part that says something, every written sentence free of placeholders, every claim (a move that takes a fact) citing a wire, no fact stated twice, and at least one claim (a text about nothing is declined). Counts are clamped: words 50-5000.

## Document types (amendment 2026-11-14 15:00)
`type` is one of `essay`, `scientific-article`, `presentation`, `story`, `report` (`DOC_TYPES` in the adapter holds only the defaults of chapters / subchapters / words: essay 3/2/600, scientific article 4/2/1200, presentation 5/0/300, story 3/0/500, report 3/2/800). The type's **procedure and style come from knowledge** (`k.genres`, `k.story`, `k.procedures`) through the library's `genre` option; the host passes the genre name only when the knowledge holds it. No free random: a random story would be "by the book" (the story method).
Commands: "write a short story", "make a presentation about the moon with 5 sections" (sections = chapters), "write a scientific article about bees, 3 chapters", plus the earlier essay forms. The words essay / composition / text / piece = essay; article / scientific article = scientific-article; presentation / slideshow; story / tale; report (`lex.docs.reader.doctypes`). The form has a Type select.

## Host
1. **Talk, "what you did"**: `refreshSummary` (`docs/webchat/app.mjs`) adds `paragraph` from `summarizer.turns(...)` (at most 3 sentences) above the structured list (`src/dialog/conversation.mjs` `summarize`, unchanged). No paragraph while unavailable.
2. **Act "summarise"** (through `summarizeText`; a document answer cites the picked sentences): `docr.digest` of docs.reader: `summarize the following: <text>` (pasted text) and `condense the document` (the open document, k sentences). The existing extractive `docr.summary` ("summarize the story", "section 2") is unchanged until the library ships; then its whole-document form should move to `std.summarize` (decision for the reviewer).
3. **Composition**: `docr.compose` with roles `topic`, `chapters`, `subchapters`, `words`. Commands (patterns in `worlds/docs.reader/patterns.extra.sop`, kinds in `lex.docs.reader.compkinds`): "write an essay about volcanoes with 3 chapters of 2 subchapters, about 600 words", "write a composition about anything", "write an essay about volcanoes", "... of 400 words". The Documents tab form (topic, chapters, subchapters, size) sends one of these commands, so the form and the command are the same path. Output is a new document (`origin: 'composed'`); `doc.provenance[sentenceIndex]` keeps the source wire and `fromOf` adds it to the answer's `from`, so "why" of a cited sentence names the wire.
4. Wiring: the engine installs the adapters in `globalThis[Symbol.for('psys2.textgen')]` when the library is loaded (`installTextGen`); the world operations (`worlds/docs.reader/textgen.mjs`) read it like the budget. The browser host creates its own adapters for the buttons (`host.textGen()`).
