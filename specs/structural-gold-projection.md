# C1 structural supervision projection

`training/sop-bert/project-structural-gold.py` processes original Haiku annotations after exact functional-development ID exclusion. It preserves every remaining row and its complete original request list in `annotations`, with source line/file/hash, source context, deterministic train/cal split, mechanically projected teacher fields and explicit unresolved decisions. It is never an inference reader or confidence provider. Raw annotated descriptor/tree heads may train broadly even when canonical binding is unresolved; absence and unresolved are distinct.

Local request/target/constraint IDs derive from list indexes. One annotated request owns its nested target and condition list; the compiler preserves that primary target rather than guessing another kind from a property prefix. Supplied selector/result phases remain separate. Unique request and child literals yield Unicode scalar ranges. Act evidence comes from the actual annotated trigger; missing/ambiguous anchors stay unresolved. Closed decisions need no invented word anchors. Open fields retain their supplied literal or declaration identity and a real source range.

Canonical lookup is separate C1 data with exact sourceWire, sourceSnapshotSHA, sourceWorld and moduleVersion on every entry. The whole lookup hash is frozen. Property entries declare token representation from the published literal/string/ref kinds; unresolved references are not fabricated. Raw property descriptors retain full area/type/key identity; generic `quantity` is not substituted for every quantity-like property. Authorial annotation-to-native joins retain their host source hashes and are reviewable independently. Semantic kind identity does not claim native host capability.

A cited unmarked-condition conjunction policy can create deterministic selector AND closure; its exact annotation-prompt hash must match. The authorial reading is that every listed condition should hold unless explicitly marked as an alternative. Explicit OR/mixed group fields remain unresolved; no group membership is invented. Rootless result lists retain the published implicit conjunction. Explicit standard bound all/any/each/none can form collection/bound targets and a quantifier only where its body and scopes are unambiguous; nonstandard/omitted quantifiers or ambiguous bodies remain unresolved. Multiple textual requests do not imply fabricated causal dependencies. Relations, relative operators, context/turn references and query conversion stay explicit until their declared transforms are available.

The teacher `labels.requests` matches the closed published `psys2.structural-predictions/1` request shape. Its outer artifact is explicitly C1 supervision, not a real model packet. Validating a teacher fixture through the decoder is serialization/ownership evidence only; it does not establish inference, semantic completeness, confidence or executable status. Original annotations remain authoritative for raw source-field training; canonical unresolved fields must not be silently replaced, clipped or interpreted as absence.

Current readiness with the first actual published-declaration lookup: all 32,267 clean rows / 36,909 requests retained; 28,959 train and 3,308 calibration rows; 1,648 development IDs excluded. Of complete rows, 611 train / 81 calibration are explicitly no-request. Only 5 nonempty training teacher graphs and zero calibration graphs are fully canonical with the current 78 kind / 2 property entries. All five pass the published decoder, with no execution claim. This is a lookup-coverage diagnostic, not permission to train five cases or discard 32,262 rows. Broad raw descriptor/structure/spans/phase supervision remains available to the structural trainer.

Full generated corpus and manifest live under local run artifacts; they are reproducible, not committed as another large generated dataset. The source-backed binding exporter is separately owned so additional declared families can be added without changing this compiler or introducing sentence rules.
