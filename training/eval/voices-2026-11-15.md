# Readers as voices, step 1: every reading path alone (2026-11-15)

Measurement only (worker-tasks 02:30 item 1): no classifier, no runtime change. `scripts/measure-voices.mjs` (`--run` English paths, `--report` tables), numbers in `voices-2026-11-15.json`. English paths are `scripts/eval-ablation.mjs` configs `v-*`, each with every other symbolic step off (typed step, alternations, near words, slot types: env switches); catalog = live sop-knowledge-worlds mirror (version b92be6741d39, publishes `w.lang.en.alternation`, 368 rules) for all paths. Romanian paths: `scripts/measure-ro-en-meaning.mjs --rows` on `ro-en-meaning/rows.jsonl` (9,328 rows with a reference reading).

Definitions. A path **answers** when its outcome is right or wrong (asked / not understood / skip is silence). Precision = right / answers; coverage = answers / n. Two paths **agree** when what they did is equal (English: visible state afterwards + answer values + effects; Romanian: the reading key of the English the path made). **Union** = any path right (oracle bound). **Agreement-only** = answer only when >= 2 voices agree. **Families** = the five symbolic paths collapse into ONE voice (first that answers) and the two neural ones into one; agreement is then between independent readers. The all-path agreement is inflated by the five correlated symbolic paths (they agree with each other by construction): read the families line.

## Caveats
- The rows of paraphrases-rt-changed are judged against the ORIGINAL expectation (robustness to a wrong translation): low precision there is expected.
- Typed form operators are learnt on the non-held-out two thirds of `form-pairs.jsonl` (run-time learning, as the other measurements); typos and loose-words rows whose reference is itself not understood are `skip` and left out of n; `junk` loose-words rows (no intent) are counted apart (`junk.false_answers` in the JSON).
- The English agreement signature is the observable result (state, values, effects), a proxy for "same act + roles"; two different readings with the same effect agree.
- The sets share rows with the learnt training material of the neural models (AGENTS.md data principle), so the neural numbers are not a held-out measure.
