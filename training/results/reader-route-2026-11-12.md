## Reading of the results (written by hand; the numbers are above and in the JSON)

- **Gate.** No right or not-understood case became a wrong answer through a reader reading, except one label mismatch: gold-real `on the new chapter` (docs.editor, voice garble of "add a new chapter"). The reader reads it as the description `addRoleBare` ("new" is a verb word of it), emits `doc.addHeading arg.role=chapter` and the reply is "OK, I started chapter 1.", the same reply the rules give to "add a new chapter"; the gold label says op `doc.newBlank`, so the checker counts it wrong. Listed as a new wrong answer, as the gate asks.
- **Gains** (rules / encoder+n1): `fill` 4 / 2 not understood to right (typos of geo.map `show` and the word `show` itself: "shw me Pariss", "Snow me Bucharest", "Show me Hyderabadd", "Show me Mins"); `choose` one more in rules (a kids.shop typo). gold-para (class b): the reader has candidates for all 14 messages of described worlds and accepts none (the paraphrases use words the descriptions do not have); loose-words (class e): it accepts none of the 105 and finds 8 ambiguous (the best-effort reader stays the source for loose words); the Romanian operational set: no change (160 of 2,480 clauses reach the reader's `choose`, none is taken: the rules' reading is as good or the library ties).
- **Why so little.** Only three worlds have a `describe.sop` (kids.shop, geo.map, docs.editor: 1,500 of about 8,400 messages) and the reader reads only the world the user is in. It is the descriptions that limit it, not the reader: the coverage of `means` is complete (table above). More descriptions (DESCRIBE-ALL) widen it without code.
- **What the first measurements taught** (section 7.4): reading every world gave 56 new wrong answers (38 not understood and 18 right turned wrong; `choose` 28 more right ones) (free-text arguments such as `doc.setTitle`, readings of a world nobody was in, "moved" read as "money"); each rule of the policy removed a named group; the policy is the result, not a tuning to the sets.
- **E2 on top.** The reader's readings add exactly the same changes with the world-aware `choose` on (rules 4 right, 1 label mismatch; encoder+n1 2 right, 1 label mismatch). The library's split candidates are read (the 'split' column of `reader-route-2026-11-12-splits.json`: 2 + 6 + 3 readings of compound messages in the operational sets and typos are splits), and a split is taken only when every frame reads; none of the changed cases above is a split.
- **Library item** (for `worker-log.md`): `interpret` of a split drops a frame it has no reading for ("mark Paris then undo": the part for "undo" is missing, `unresolved` and `words` are empty), so `rank` and `veto` cannot see the word left out; the host counts frames against parts.
