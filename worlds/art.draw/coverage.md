# art.draw: request-shape coverage (R2)

Every line is a request shape of one operation family. `supported` lines are covered by a test in `tests.sop` (ids `t.art.draw.r2<group>NN`, generated by running the line on the real engine: the reply start and the changed state are asserted). `unsupported` lines say why. Shapes: imperative, question, wish ("I want"), indirect ("it should be", "X would be nicer"), elliptic (very short), voice (fillers, run-ons), typo/abbreviation (u, pls, thx, dk, grn, whats), word-order, synonym, pronoun, quantity, negation, coordination, politeness, state questions and option questions. The general mechanisms behind them: a filler + politeness prefix (`um so like`, `hey yo could you`, `i was wondering if you could`), a trailer (`pls thx ok i guess ish`), noun lexicons (`targets`, `parts`, `preps`, `regions`, `flipwords`), and ops that accept extra adjectives for noun-first moves.

**L7 (macros, extends, shapes).** `patterns.sop` was migrated with `scripts/migrate-macros.mjs` (lead-ins, suffixes and 18 repeated lists are macros); `lex.art.draw.mods` extends `lex.lang.size` and `lex.art.draw.ordinals` extends `lex.lang.ordinal`. Two shapes (9 generated patterns) cover `draw.ground` ("could you maybe draw some grass on the ground", "i want to add sand": do family over `lex.art.draw.ground`) and `draw.mood` ("could you maybe make it into night": set family, noun-only, over `lex.art.draw.times`); tests sh01 to sh03. Seven hand patterns whose examples a shape covered completely were deleted. A third shape for `draw.colorOf` ("what color is my house") was tried and dropped: its free-text object (`words(5)`) cannot require the word "my", and without it the shape read "what color is a carrot" in kids.shapes (7 routed heldout rows lost), so `color.my`, `color.in`, `color.of` stay. Adding, recolouring, moving, counting, describing, saving and the RD4 comparatives and people presets take object phrases (`words`) with many modifiers and stay hand-written.
